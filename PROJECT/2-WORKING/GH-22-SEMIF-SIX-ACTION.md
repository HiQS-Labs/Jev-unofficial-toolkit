Material entry points and writes:

- Needle `spike/coding_core/prepare_openhands.py` owns the frozen split and q1 serialization; `baselines.py` owns the trivial references.

- This repository already owns metric calculation in `jev/eval.py` and safe create-only result writing in `jev/guard.py`; the experiment extends those rather than adding parallel metric or persistence subsystems.
- Raw rows, model caches, raw SemIf output, and source issue text remain outside Git. Only typed projections and hashes enter `evidence/2026-09-22-semif-six-action/`.

## Acceptance checks

- Exact frozen-data and baseline preflight passes before model loading.
- One canonical run scores exactly 100 sequential IDs with no skips or option drift.
- The committed result contains no state/query/issue text, machine-local path, cache, model weights, or raw third-party trajectory.
- A separate verification path reproduces top-1, macro-F1, confusion, per-label counts, and probability buckets from the committed typed projections.
- Receipt names exact code/model/data revisions and distinguishes conditional option probability from calibrated confidence.
- Focused tests, repository full gate, SemIf checks, and final relay QA are green; failures remain reported as failures.

## Risks, rollback, and non-goals

- Resource pressure: source-precision MLX may exceed comfortable unified memory. Stop and report; do not silently quantize the canonical arm.
- Repeated-sample interpretation: the labels are already known and the sample has two trajectories with zero `git` support. State those limits prominently.
- External drift: every relevant revision and generated holdout hash is pinned; mismatch is a stop condition.
- Rollback is deletion of the additive receipt/runner/test plus the two documentation pointers. No runtime interface, schema, dependency, or production behavior changes.
- Non-goals: SemIf source changes, an adapter, training, calibration fitting, wording/rich-state ablations, Jev replay, private data, deployment, or a broader model-quality claim.
